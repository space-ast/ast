SpaceAST

Modern C++ algorithms for aerospace simulation

Get startedModule referenceAPI referenceDownload

High-precision orbit propagation

Two-body, J2/J4 analytical and HPOP numerical propagation with configurable force models.

Complete frames and time system

J2000/ECEF transformation chains, Julian date and UTC/TAI/TT conversions, JPL DE and SPICE SPK ephemerides.

Attitude and math foundations

Quaternions, Euler angles, DCMs, linear algebra, ODE integrators and interpolation.

Modular shared libraries

core, math and util can be linked independently with clear dependencies.

Cross-platform builds

Windows and Linux, C++11, with xmake or CMake integration.

Python bindings

SWIG-generated bindings for scripting, validation and rapid prototyping.
